HOW TO PRINT
------------
1. Pick the file that matches your frame’s aspect ratio.
2. Open it in your favorite photo printer (Mpix, Shutterfly,
   Printique, Walmart Photo) or your local print shop
   (Staples, FedEx Office, Walgreens, CVS, Costco).
3. Choose “fit to page” and your size; matte or lustre paper
   gives the richest blacks for this image.

FRAME NOT INCLUDED — print first, then frame in any size you love.
